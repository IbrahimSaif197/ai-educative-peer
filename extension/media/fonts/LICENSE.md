Both faces are used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Each font's own
licence text is bundled here rather than the generic OFL template, so the
copyright holder is named:

- **Bricolage Grotesque** (700, latin subset) — full licence in
  `OFL-bricolage.txt`. Copyright 2022 The Bricolage Grotesque Project
  Authors. https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage
- **Geist** (variable, latin subset) — full licence in `OFL-geist.txt`.
  Copyright (c) 2023 Vercel, in collaboration with basement.studio.
  https://github.com/vercel/geist-font

Subsets were taken from the Google Fonts CDN and are shipped locally so the
webview renders with no network access.
