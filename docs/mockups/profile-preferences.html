Why the ring

  • The audit's A6 says “hint 4 ●●●● is a score with no scale.” Four arcs are a scale. Two lit out of four reads as depth without a legend.
  • It reuses a shape the panel already owns — .brand__mark is described in the CSS as “a filled ring: the tutor is present but never the loudest thing on screen.”
  • It makes the avatar earn its place. A plain circle with initials is chrome; this one is the only always-visible readout of the thing EduPeer is actually about.
  • Signed out, no arc is lit and a coral pip appears. The state reads at a glance and the call to action is not a fourth button in the header.

Behaviour

  1. Click, Enter or Space opens. Esc, a click outside, or blur closes it and returns focus to the avatar.
  2. Arrow keys move between rows; the popover is a role="menu" with aria-expanded on the avatar.
  3. Toggles apply immediately — no Save. Each posts to the extension host, which calls workspace.getConfiguration().update(…, Global).
  4. A settings change made anywhere else (Settings UI, another window) repaints the popover, so it never shows a stale value.
  5. Opens with a 140 ms fade and a 4 px rise on --ease-signature; prefers-reduced-motion drops it to a plain fade.
  6. “Start a fresh session” swaps the popover's last group for the confirm strip in place, rather than opening a modal over a 400 px panel.

What shipped differently

  • No second avatar in the popover. States 2 and 3 above show one in the identity block, which reads well on this page because the two are far apart. In the panel the popover opens about 30 px under the header avatar, so both rings are on screen at once showing identical state. One of them is decoration; the header keeps it.
  • The footer Reset routes here too. It opens this popover with the confirm already showing rather than firing. Leaving it to fire on the first click while the popover asked first would have made the confirm decorative — and it is the button sitting a pixel from Ask.
  • role="dialog", not role="menu". A menu's children must all be menu items; this one has a heading, an identity block and a stepper. The toggles are role="switch", which is both valid here and the accurate name for them.
